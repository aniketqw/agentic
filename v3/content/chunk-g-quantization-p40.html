Plain unweighted weight MSE tells the opposite story here: naive = 0.00067 vs. GPTQ = 0.00137 — GPTQ's weights are individually less accurate on average.
That's not a bug: GPTQ deliberately pushes weights further from their true values in directions H says the output barely notices, in exchange for staying
accurate in directions the output is sensitive to. It optimizes what the layer actually computes, not how close the weights look on paper.
