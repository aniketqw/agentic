The gradient, qualitatively
The Policy Gradient Theorem gives an update rule with the shape every policy-gradient method shares: push up the probability of an action in proportion to a
reward signal, push it down otherwise. Here, at each generated token yt , the reward is Rt — how much more the teacher likes the rest of this response, from this
​

​

point on, than the student itself currently expects.
