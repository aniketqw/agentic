    A negative corpus result has a domain of validity

    The DeepSeekMath slide reports no improvement, sometimes deterioration, for the tested models on arXiv-only mathematical corpora. The authors scope this as preliminary. It does not establish that formal papers are useless: scale, filtering, mixture composition, continued-training budget, and evaluation task can all matter. A useful hypothesis is “this data mixture and these models did not improve these evaluations,” rather than “arXiv cannot teach reasoning.”

    A point change subtracts values on the same metric scale; it is not a relative percent improvement and not a cross-metric average. Sequential stages inherit previous weights and data. Their observed differences help describe the pipeline but do not identify every causal ingredient without controlled ablations.
