F.5 Limits of this verification

  • Numbers were checked against each slide's text layer. Some slides carry tables as images, so those numbers could not be checked automatically.
  • Arithmetic was checked only where an equation was purely numeric (594 cases). Calculations involving matrices, vectors or symbols were not recomputed.
  • Explanations (as opposed to numbers) were checked by sampling and by the source-citation tests above, not line by line.
  • Where the chat stated a fact from outside your materials (for example IndicSentiment's per-language test size), it was not flagged unless it contradicted a source.
