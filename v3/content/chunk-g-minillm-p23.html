Training MiniLLM: two phases
1. Phase 1 — SFT initialization. Fine-tune the student on the labeled instruction-response dataset D , standard supervised training. This gives policy gradient a

reasonable starting point instead of a random one.
2. Phase 2 — MiniLLM refinement. Continue training with the combined gradient from the three fixes above (single-step + teacher-mixed + length-normalized),

alongside a language-modeling loss on a separate pretraining corpus DPT to preserve the student's general capabilities.
​

The overall pipeline mirrors RLHF: an SFT phase followed by a policy-optimization phase — except the reward here comes directly from the teacher's own output
distribution, not a separately-trained reward model.
