Decoding a float: a worked example
Take the FP16 bit pattern below and recover the number it encodes, one field at a time:

ST E P

CO M P U TATI O N

R E S U LT

sign

S = 0 ⇒ (−1)0

+1

exponent

E = 100002 = 16, bias = 24 − 1 = 15

16 − 15 = 1

mantissa

bits 1001001000 ⇒ 2−1 + 2−4 + 2−7

0.5703125

value

(+1) × 21 × (1 + 0.5703125)

3.140625
