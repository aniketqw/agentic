What this means for training

Sequence-level KD samples y from the teacher and trains the student to match those exact samples — forward KLD, forcing memorization of everything the
teacher might generate. MiniLLM samples y from the student itself and asks the teacher to grade it — reverse KLD, improving the text the student already tends to
produce rather than forcing it to reproduce the teacher's.
