Lower exposure bias

Teacher-forced training only ever sees ground-truth prefixes, never its own mistakes — at free-run inference, small errors compound as generation gets longer
(exposure bias). MiniLLM trains on the student's own generated rollouts (reverse KLD samples y ∼ qθ ), so it already sees, and corrects for, its own mistakes during
​

training — the excess error (ExAccErr) stops growing past ~150 tokens, while the baselines keep drifting.
