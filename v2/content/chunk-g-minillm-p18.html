Seeing it directly: fitting a single Gaussian
Fit one Gaussian q to a multi-modal target p (a Gaussian mixture) by minimizing each KLD direction:

Forward KLD (orange) spreads itself thin trying to cover every mode at once, ending up low and wide — reasonably present everywhere, confidently right nowhere.
Reverse KLD (green) picks the single largest mode and matches it closely, ignoring the smaller ones entirely — exactly the mode-seeking behavior from the

previous slide, now visible on an actual distribution.
