Does mode-seeking hurt diversity?
Reverse KLD is mode-seeking by design — a natural worry is that it makes generations repetitive. Measured directly with distinct-4-grams (Dist-4, higher = more
diverse vocabulary) and language-modeling loss on held-out text:

MiniLLM's Dist-4 and loss stay close to the teacher's and the other baselines' — mode-seeking within one response does not collapse diversity across different
prompts or different sampling seeds.
