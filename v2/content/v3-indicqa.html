Explain why some questions are unanswerable

In the preceding IndicQA construction slides, one phase creates questions from a broader context and a later phase marks answers in a restricted context. A question can therefore be valid yet unanswerable from the passage the model receives. The benchmark tests evidence-grounded abstention as well as span extraction; a plausible answer supplied from memory can still violate the task.

Annotation practices are part of data quality

Slide 27 reports native-speaker annotators, compensation based on qualifications/experience/local norms, disclosure that annotations would be public, screening for personal/offensive content, MIT code/model licensing, and CC-0 datasets. Preserve these as source-reported practices for this release. They are not a blanket licensing or consent statement about every future derivative dataset.

The connection to evaluation is concrete: native-language expertise affects annotation validity; review procedures affect label reliability; privacy screening affects which passages are retained; release terms determine permitted reuse. Discuss these alongside metrics rather than treating them as decorative metadata. Aggregate performance still does not show that every language or dialect is equally served.
