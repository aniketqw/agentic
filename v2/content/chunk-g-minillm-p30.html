Bigger teachers make better students

Fix the student (GPT-2-125M) and grow only the teacher. Prior work has found this doesn't always help — a bigger teacher can even hurt naive distillation. MiniLLM
instead improves monotonically with teacher size, while SeqKD stays flat — reverse KLD keeps extracting more signal from a stronger teacher instead of just
inheriting its blind spots.
