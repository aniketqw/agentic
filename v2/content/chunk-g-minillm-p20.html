Why this needs policy gradient
KL[qθ ∥p] is an expectation over y ∼ qθ — the distribution being optimized is also the one doing the sampling. Ordinary backprop can't differentiate through a
​

​

sampling step directly. This is exactly the situation reinforcement learning solves: treat the student as a policy choosing tokens, use the Policy Gradient Theorem
to get a usable gradient anyway.
THE RL FRAMING

State = the prompt plus tokens generated so far, (x, y<t ). Action = the next token yt , chosen from the vocabulary. Reward = how much more the teacher likes
​

each generated token than the student already does.
